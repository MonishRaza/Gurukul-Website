Put It on Your Home Screen

Optional — but this gives the app its own icon on your phone, just like an installed app:

  1. Open the app in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right corner).
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” or “Install app”.
  4. Confirm — an app icon now sits on your home screen and opens the app full-screen.

On iPhone: open in Safari → tap the Share button → “Add to Home Screen”. Having trouble? Message us on WhatsApp from the contact page.
