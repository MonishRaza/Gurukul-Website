About Our School

A journey of learning that began in 2013 — and is still growing.

Our Story

Gurukul Academy Higher Secondary School was founded in 2013 on Satna Road in Village Gadauli, Amarpatan (District Maihar, Madhya Pradesh), with a simple vision: to give children an education rooted in discipline and values, while preparing them fully for the modern world.

From our earliest batches to today, the school has grown with its students — building classrooms, laboratories and a culture of care. More than a decade later, our alumni carry the Gurukul spirit into colleges, careers and communities.

Our Mission

To nurture every student into a confident, responsible and capable individual — combining strong academics with moral character, so that each child leaves us ready to contribute to family, community and country.
