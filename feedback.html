Share Your Feedback

Suggestions, appreciation, concerns or questions — every message reaches the school directly.

One-time setup needed

This form sends messages to the school's email through FormSubmit (free). To switch it on:

  1. Put the school's real email in js/site-config.js (the email: line).
  2. Submit this form once — FormSubmit emails a confirmation link to that address.
  3. Click the link. Done — all future messages arrive in the school inbox.

Full details are in README.md.

Your message goes straight to the school's email inbox. You can also reach us instantly on WhatsApp.
